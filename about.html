EXPOSURE TRIANGLE /EV 00
The three mechanical and electronic pillars governing optical reciprocity, depth, and photon volume:
01. APERTURE (f-stop)
Diameter of the diaphragm. Controls both the volume of light entering the chamber and the selective optical depth of field (plane of critical focus).
02. SHUTTER SPEED (sec)
Physical duration the focal curtain stays open. Governs motion temporal registration (freezing high velocity vs. cinematic motion blur).
03. ISO (gain)
Base sensitivity amplification of the recording medium. Balances photon starvation in low light against analog grain and luminance noise.
